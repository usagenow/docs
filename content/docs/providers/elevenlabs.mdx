title: ElevenLabs
description: Monthly characters and subscription allowance from ElevenLabs' official read-only subscription endpoint.
---

Available in **0.8.0** through the official subscription API.

## Connect

1. Create a dedicated restricted API key in your ElevenLabs account with **user_read** permission.
2. Enable ElevenLabs under **Settings › Providers**, then choose **Connect**.
3. Paste the key and choose **Check and Save**.

A missing permission is reported separately from a rejected key. UsageNow makes no audio-generation request and does not spend characters to verify the connection.

## What it shows

- Characters used and the monthly character allowance.
- The plan when reported.
- A reset date only when the subscription response explicitly provides one.

Characters are not tokens and are not labeled as today's local activity. Missing fields are not estimated.

## Source and storage

`GET https://api.elevenlabs.io/v1/user/subscription`

The key is stored only in this Mac's Keychain, sent only to `api.elevenlabs.io`, and deleted when you remove the connection or turn the provider off. See [Connect a provider](/providers/connections) for additional accounts and request cooldowns.
