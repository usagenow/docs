Available in **1.1.0** for **global M Plan** subscriptions.

<Callout title="Live account validation pending">
The integration has automated checks against the documented API and official CLI schema. A real paid M Plan account has not yet been used to validate it. PAYG balances and regional plans are outside this integration.
</Callout>

## Connect

1. Open your global MiniMax account and obtain its **M Plan subscription key**.
2. Enable MiniMax in **Settings › Providers**, then choose **Connect**.
3. Enter the key and choose **Check and Save**.

A successful read-only quota request is required before the key is saved. UsageNow does not sign in to MiniMax, import browser credentials, generate content, or refresh your key.

## What it shows

- The quota windows and reset times MiniMax reports.
- Remaining usage only when the response provides consistent counters or percentages.
- Separate accounts through **Add Account…**, with their own keys.
- The same reported limits in the menu bar and widget.

Unlimited weekly limits are omitted. Missing data and ambiguous boosted-plan denominators remain unknown rather than becoming a made-up percentage. Local token activity is not available through this connection.

## Source and storage

The official read-only source is:

    GET https://www.minimax.io/v1/token_plan/remains

The subscription key is stored only in UsageNow's Keychain on this Mac and sent only to **www.minimax.io**. Redirects are refused. Removing an account or turning MiniMax off deletes the key saved in UsageNow. It never changes MiniMax's own credentials.

See [connection checks and cooldowns](/providers/connections) for rejected keys, HTTP 429 and stale readings.
