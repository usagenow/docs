UsageNow includes native macOS widgets, built with WidgetKit, that you can place on your desktop.

## Sizes

### Small

A glance at what matters most: for each enabled provider, the usage window with the least left, plus the next reset. With a single provider, it has room for the window’s name and reset time.

### Medium

A column for each enabled provider, with its usage windows, the percentage left in each, and reset times. With three or more providers, it switches to one compact row per provider so each stays readable. When more providers are enabled than fit, the ones closest to a limit are shown.

In both sizes, percentages follow **Settings › General › Show limits as**: what's **left** by default, or what's **used** — exactly as in the [menu bar](/menu-bar#left-or-used).

A provider with no limits to show — Gemini CLI, Warp, OpenCode, Qoder, Qwen Code, Cline, or Grok Build — shows today's activity in its own unit instead: tokens, or credits for Kiro, Warp, and Qoder. In the medium widget's rows, such a provider also gets a small chart of its last two weeks, one bar per day with today's bar highlighted. The chart shows the shape of your activity, not amounts, and turns off with **Settings › General › Show the last 30 days**.

An account you [added](/settings#accounts) appears as its own row, named after the provider and the account — **Codex · Work**.

## Add the widget

1. Right-click an empty area of your desktop and choose **Edit Widgets**.
2. Search for **UsageNow**.
3. Choose Small or Medium and drag it onto your desktop.

Open UsageNow at least once first, so the widget has data to show.

## How the widget gets its data

The widget never reads your provider data itself.

```text
UsageNow app ──► sanitized snapshot (App Group) ──► widget
```

The main UsageNow app refreshes providers, then writes a small snapshot of the values the widget displays to a shared App Group container on your Mac. The widget renders only that snapshot.

<Callout type="privacy" title="The widget is isolated">
  The widget doesn’t access `~/.codex` or `~/.claude`, doesn’t open the Keychain, doesn’t call Anthropic, and doesn’t launch Codex. The code that talks to providers and credentials isn’t part of the widget at all.
</Callout>

The snapshot holds display values only: each enabled provider’s usage windows and reset times, plan, most recent model, today’s token, request, and credit counts, the name you gave an added account, the shape of the last two weeks as shares of the busiest day, and whether percentages read as left or used. It never contains prompts, code, file paths, or credentials. It stays on your Mac.

## Providers and the widget

The widget follows your provider settings automatically:

- Providers you turn off in **Settings › Providers** don’t appear in the widget.
- Providers that aren’t installed don’t appear either.
- When a provider has no usage limits available, the widget shows **Usage limits unavailable** instead of a number.

## Freshness

The widget shows what UsageNow last saw. Its values change when the UsageNow app refreshes and writes a new snapshot, so keep UsageNow running if you rely on the widget. [Launch at Login](/settings#general) helps.

- When the data is old, the widget keeps showing the last known values with an **Updated … ago** note rather than hiding them.
- The widget asks macOS to redraw it periodically and shortly after a reset, so countdowns stay current. macOS decides exactly when widgets update.
- While UsageNow updates, the app and the widget can briefly be different versions. The widget skips anything from a newer app it doesn't recognize — a provider added in that version, for example — and shows the rest.

## Widget messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Open UsageNow to load usage data.** | The widget has no snapshot yet. Open UsageNow, or use a build with widget support. |
| **No providers enabled** | Every provider is turned off. Open UsageNow to choose providers. |
| **No providers detected** | None of your enabled providers is installed on this Mac. |
| **Usage limits unavailable** | A provider isn’t reporting limits right now. |

See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#widget-shows-old-data) if the widget looks stale or doesn’t appear.
